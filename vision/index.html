Why we exist
02 / 10

Problems worth investigating with communities.

Unequal access to learning

Who lacks access to quality learning, skills, and opportunities, and why?

Stretched frontline workers

Where do educators and social workers lack practical digital support?

Tools that miss the context

Which digital tools are not designed for local languages, devices, and needs?

Fragmented public-interest tech

Where is useful technology rebuilt repeatedly instead of shared and reused?

These are questions to investigate and validate, not established findings. We will test each with the people affected.

Our vision
03 / 10

Technology that people can access, understand, and shape.

We want to help create a future where useful technology is built with communities, not only for them, and where responsible AI supports educators and social workers rather than replacing them.

Accessible

Usable across devices, languages, and abilities.

Community-centered

Needs guide every technology decision.

Responsible

Privacy-conscious AI with people in control.

Open

Shared tools and learning resources where possible.

Four impact pillars
04 / 10

One mission. Four pillars.

01 · First Focus

Education and Learning

Tools and resources that improve access to learning, skills, knowledge, and opportunities.

02 · Pillar

Social Impact and Community Empowerment

Technology that helps communities, social workers, educators, and grassroots organizations address real challenges.

03 · Pillar

Responsible and Accessible AI

Human-centered AI that is useful, understandable, accessible, and appropriate for underserved communities.

04 · Pillar

Open Technology and Sustainability

Open-source tools, reusable infrastructure, public learning resources, and sustainable development approaches.

Many opportunities to create public good sit under these pillars.
