The story behind the name

Inspired by a mother. Built around possibility.

Babunty Devi Sahani is my mother. This initiative carries her name as a reminder that meaningful change can begin with care for another person and the willingness to act.

We are starting small, without an institution, a team or a funding pool behind us. What we do have is the intention to contribute time, skills and effort towards better learning opportunities.

We don't want to pretend to be bigger than we are. We want to begin honestly, learn from people and grow only when we can do so responsibly.

What we care about

Start with learning. Keep the door open.

Our first area of focus is quality education and practical learning. Over time, we hope to explore other ways to support people and communities—guided by real needs, not promises on a page.

01 / ACCESS

Learning for all

Making useful learning resources easier to discover and access, especially for people with fewer opportunities.

02 / SKILLS

Skills for life

Encouraging digital literacy, communication, problem-solving and practical skills that help people move forward.

03 / POSSIBILITY

Room to grow

Exploring simple tools, projects and collaborations that help learners build confidence and take their next step.

How we want to work

Do what we can. Be clear about what we can't.

We believe trust is built by being transparent about our stage, our capacity and our progress. We will share real work as it happens—not invented numbers or claims of impact.

1

Listen first

Understand what learners, families and educators actually need before designing solutions.

2

Build simply

Use time, skills and accessible technology to create useful learning resources and small experiments.

3

Grow responsibly

Work with others, measure what is real and take formal steps before expanding into activities that require them.

A note on our current status

We are at the beginning. And we want to be honest about it.

Babunty Devi Sahani Foundation is currently an early-stage, independent initiative and is not yet registered as a trust, society, Section 8 company or other charitable organisation in India. We are not claiming tax-exempt status, and we are not soliciting donations at this stage. Any future programmes, partnerships or fundraising will be considered only when the appropriate structure and safeguards are in place.
