About the Foundation

Rooted in Compassion, Driven by Purpose

Founded with an enduring commitment to social equity and humanitarian care, the foundation serves as a catalyst for meaningful change.

Our Legacy

Honoring the Spirit of Babunty Devi Sahani

The foundation carries forward the benevolent spirit, kindness, and visionary empathy exemplified by Babunty Devi Sahani. Her lifelong belief that every human being deserves fair opportunities, quality care, and a life of dignity is the guiding compass for everything we do.

We believe in lasting, grassroots empowerment that helps families stand on their own feet, educate their children, and build self-reliant, resilient communities.

"True progress begins when the most vulnerable among us are uplifted with dignity and hope."

Our Mission

To provide accessible educational opportunities, health welfare assistance, and vocational skill training to marginalized and underserved communities.

Our Vision

A compassionate, equitable society where no child drops out due to hardship, no family suffers for lack of basic healthcare, and every woman can achieve financial independence.

Key Initiatives

Where We Create Lasting Impact

Our multi-pronged programs address critical social needs at the root level.

Pillar 01

Education & Youth Literacy

Providing school supply kits, learning aids, and student sponsorships so children from economically challenged backgrounds can stay in school and excel.

  • Child learning kits & books
  • Scholarship assistance programs
  • Foundational literacy & digital skills
Pillar 02

Healthcare & Wellness Outreach

Organizing preventive health screening, medicine distribution, eye camps, and maternal wellness awareness in underserved settlements.

  • Free medical check-up camps
  • Preventive health & hygiene kits
  • Emergency medical assistance
Pillar 03

Women Empowerment & Skills

Empowering women through vocational training, financial literacy workshops, and small-enterprise mentoring to enable sustainable self-reliance.

  • Vocational craft & sewing programs
  • Financial literacy & bank access
  • Self-help group leadership
Pillar 04

Grassroots Community Care

Standing with communities during seasonal hardships and natural distress through food ration distribution, clean water access, and relief work.

  • Essential food & ration kits
  • Seasonal clothing & blanket drives
  • Grassroots community welfare
Guiding Principles

The Values That Define Us

01

Compassion First

Every individual is treated with warmth, empathy, and unconditional dignity.

02

Absolute Transparency

We are accountable to our community and ensure every contribution reaches those in need.

03

Sustainable Impact

We build programs that empower people to achieve long-term independence, not temporary dependency.

04

Inclusivity & Equity

Aid and support are offered universally without discrimination of caste, creed, or background.
